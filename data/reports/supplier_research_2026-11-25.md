# Football data suppliers, researched and critic-verified 2026-11-25

Need: per-match xG (shot-level better), lineups and injuries before kickoff, fixtures/results, the five leagues, 5+ seasons, REST API, terms allowing a paid site. Each supplier was researched from live pages and re-checked by a second agent.

## Sportmonks Football API (sportmonks.com) — self-serve tiers Free / Starter / Growth / Pro / Enterprise, confirmed live on 2026-11-25 at /football-api/plans-pricing. xG is NOT in any base plan: it is sold only as the 'xG Basic' (post-match) or 'xG Advanced' (live) add-on (or an 'xG & Pressure Index' bundle), priced per tier. The '6 March 2026 pricing relaunch' date was not re-checked by the critic.

**Recommended plan:** Starter (5 leagues) + xG Basic add-on + one-time Historical data add-on: €48/month paid monthly (€29 + €19) or ~€39/month paid yearly (€24 + €15), all ex-VAT, plus a one-time historical fee 'starting at €29' — prices confirmed live. Cheapest tier that gives per-match team+player xG (post-match), confirmed official lineups ~1h pre-kickoff with a lineup_confirmed flag, injury/suspension records (via sidelined.sideline) and live fixtures/results for exactly EPL/La Liga/Serie A/Bundesliga/Ligue 1. It does NOT satisfy need 1 fully (no shot-level xG/coordinates on any tier) nor need 5 for xG (xG only from 2024/25 and not guaranteed complete even then); pre-1h predicted lineups are impossible on Starter (Expected Lineups add-on is Growth+ only, €199/month).

**Verdict:** verified_by_critic: Yes — fetched the live pricing page, docs xG coverage page, xg-data page, lineups guide, sidelined guide, rate-limit docs, Expected Lineups docs, FAQ, ToS and Premier League page on 2026-11-25; the recommended plan's prices (€29/€24 base, €19/€15 xG Basic, €24 bundle), xG scope (13 team/player metrics, no shot-level, 2024/25 onward) and lineup/injury availability (official lineups ~1h pre-kickoff in all plans; Expected Lineups €199 Growth+ only; sidelined free on fixtures/teams) all check out. Partial fit, and cheap (~€39-48/month ex-VAT), with the same gaps as reported: no shot-level xG or coordinates anywhere at Sportmonks, xG history only from 2024/25 (not enough for 5+ seasons of xG backtesting, and completeness per competition is not guaranteed), seasons older than three cost an unpriced one-time add-on, injury-update cadence is undocumented, and early predicted XIs need a Growth plan plus €199/month. Corrections made: the sidelined include needs the nested 'sidelined.sideline' chain to expose start/end dates and games missed; the FAQ's 'free to use for commercial purposes' line is Copa-America-specific, not a general statement, so ToS 6.3 ('personal and non-commercial purposes', under the IP-rights article) stands unresolved and written confirmation is required before a paid site launches; the '€49 xG on Enterprise' figure could not be found on the cited blog.

## API-Football (API-Sports) — api-football.com / api-sports.io (base URL v3.football.api-sports.io)

**Recommended plan:** none if need 1 means shot-level xG with coordinates (no plan has it). If team-level match xG is acceptable, or xG is sourced elsewhere, Pro is the cheapest plan covering needs 2-5: $19/month or $159.60 prepaid for 12 months ex VAT per the latest verifiable official figures (researcher's $16 / $134.40 unconfirmed) — but run a live test of fixtures/statistics on a current top-5 fixture before paying, to see whether expected_goals is actually returned.

**Verdict:** verified_by_critic: live supplier pages were unreachable to automated fetching (Cloudflare challenge, not bypassed), so verification used the supplier's own pages via Internet Archive captures (pricing 2026-03-14, homepage 2026-06-24, api-sports.io 2026-03-11, coverage 2026-04-10, OpenAPI spec 2025-11-02) plus third-party pages.
