🔬 The model — what will happen

Produces a probability for every fixture. Judged by calibration: did the things it called 32% happen about 32% of the time. A 32% pick that loses is the model working, not failing — it never claimed more. Lives in Predictions, History, Table.

🎰 The betting — what money is on

Turns some of those probabilities into stakes at a price, in a structure. Judged by P&L. One bet cycle per round: a single שיטה slip plus the GOLD measurement pot — never a bet on every pick. Lives in Ledger and Monkey 🐒.

The two disagree on purpose: a losing draw pick can still be a good bet (the price paid for a 32% event), and a winning favourite can still be a bad bet (backed below its breakeven). Every pick below carries be = the minimum price worth taking. Below that number, don't bet it — no structure turns a bad price into a good one.
